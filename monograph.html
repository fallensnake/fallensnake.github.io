§ I · Premise

An interdisciplinary operator.

I am a third-year double major in Analytics (Operations Research & Management Science) and Applied Mathematics, with a minor in Data Science. I am drawn to the moments where technical depth and business judgment overlap — where a Bayesian update is also a strategic decision, and a constraint is also a value statement.

Last summer at Berkeley Skydeck, I built a Bayesian Belief Network of over 1,000 variables to model success probabilities for low-income international students entering top-tier universities. I used convex optimization to improve the Bayesian update by 40%, then layered root-cause analysis on top — so resources could flow to the weak edges of the graph, not the loud ones.

The thing I keep optimizing for is leverage: where a single well-formulated objective function moves the most lives at once. I think that's where the technical-and-business mixture earns its keep.
